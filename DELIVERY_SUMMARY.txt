💡 UNDERSTANDING THE SIGNALS
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

OVERSOLD (RSI < 30 or Stochastic < 20%)
  → Price dropped sharply
  → Potential bounce expected
  → Good BUY opportunity
  → Recommendation: BUY
  → Stop Loss: 2.5%

OVERBOUGHT (RSI > 70 or Stochastic > 80%)
  → Price rallied sharply
  → Potential pullback expected
  → Good SELL opportunity
  → Recommendation: SELL
  → Stop Loss: 2.5%

NEUTRAL (Everything else)
  → No extreme condition
  → Wait for clearer signal
  → Recommendation: HOLD
  → Stop Loss: 1.5% (safer)
