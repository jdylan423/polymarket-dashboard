## 3. COMPLETE RISK ASSESSMENT

### Risk Matrix with Mitigation Strategies

| Risk Category | Severity | Probability | Impact | Mitigation Strategy |
|---------------|----------|-------------|--------|---------------------|
| **Utility Cost Shocks** | High | Medium | 3-8% margin compression | Lock fixed-rate contracts; invest in high-efficiency equipment; pass-through pricing (90-120 day lag) |
| **Equipment Failure** | Medium | Medium | $5K-$25K unplanned expense | Preventive maintenance program; IoT monitoring; equipment reserves |
| **Competition (New Entrants)** | Medium | Low | 10-20% revenue erosion | Differentiate with tech/service; loyalty programs; premium positioning |
| **Suburban In-Home Washer Penetration** | Medium | High | Gradual demand decline | Focus on urban/high-density markets only |
| **Regulatory (Water/Wastewater)** | Medium | Medium | $10K-$50K compliance costs | Pre-acquisition regulatory audit; factor into deal structure |
| **Theft/Vandalism** | Low | Medium | $500-$2K monthly | Cashless systems (>90% adoption); security cameras; remote monitoring |
| **Labor Shortage** (Full-Service) | Medium | Medium | Service degradation | Competitive wages; streamline operations; automate where possible |
| **Economic Recession** | Low | Low | 5-10% revenue dip | Essential service = recession-resistant; 95% customer retention |

### Operational Risk Details

**1. Rising Utility Costs (HIGHEST PRIORITY)**
- **Water/Sewer:** 12-20% of sales (largest variable expense)
- **Electricity:** Dryer/HVAC costs rising 3-5% annually
- **Gas:** Hot water heating (if gas-fired systems)
- **Mitigation:** 
  - Negotiate utility contracts (fixed rates where available)
  - ENERGY STAR equipment (40% lower water usage by 2026 EPA standards)
  - High G-force extractors (reduce drying time 20-30%)
  - Price increases lag costs by 90-120 days (plan accordingly)

**2. Equipment Maintenance & Replacement**
- **Lifespan:** Commercial washers/dryers last 8-14 years
- **Annual Maintenance:** 2-6% of revenue
- **Replacement Cost:** $1,500-$7,500 per machine (washers), $1,200-$5,000 (dryers)
- **Mitigation:**
  - Establish equipment reserve fund (5% of revenue annually)
  - IoT-connected machines reduce downtime 20-30%
  - Service contracts with local distributors (faster response times)

**3. Regulatory Compliance**
- **Wastewater Regulations:** Stricter limits in California, EU-standard states
- **Permitting Timeline:** 90-180 days average (California: 180+ days)
- **Operating Hour Restrictions:** Some municipalities limit 24/7 operations
- **ADA Compliance:** Required for public facilities
- **Mitigation:**
  - Hire local attorney for pre-acquisition regulatory review
  - Budget $5K-$15K for compliance in offer price
  - Join local laundromat association (regulatory advocacy)

**4. Market/Competitive Risks**
- **New Construction:** Apartments with in-unit washers erode suburban demand
- **Delivery Services:** Venture-backed competitors with aggressive pricing
- **Mitigation:**
  - Urban-only investment thesis (avoid suburban greenfield)
  - Differentiate: technology integration, loyalty programs, premium service
  - Multi-location strategy (economies of scale)

### Black Swan Events

| Event | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation |
|-------|-----------|--------|------------|
| Pandemic-level disruption | Very Low | 20-40% revenue drop | Essential service status (stayed open 2020-2021); delivery pivot |
| Extreme weather/flooding | Low | $50K-$200K damage | Flood insurance; elevated equipment; emergency backup power |
| Major utility rate shock (>25%) | Low | 8-15% margin hit | Immediate pricing adjustment; customer communication; efficiency upgrades |

## APPENDIX B: VALUATION CALCULATOR

Use this formula to quickly assess any listing:

**Step 1: Calculate SDE (Seller's Discretionary Earnings)**
```
SDE = Net Income + Owner Salary + Interest + Depreciation + One-Time Expenses
```

**Step 2: Apply Appropriate Multiple**
- New equipment (0-5 years): 4.5-5.5x SDE
- Mid-age equipment (6-12 years): 4.0-4.5x SDE
- Older equipment (13-16 years): 3.5-4.0x SDE
- Old equipment (17+ years): 2.5-3.5x SDE (factor in replacement costs)

**Step 3: Adjust for Market Factors**
- **Add 10-20%:** Premium urban market (NYC, LA, SF, Chicago)
- **Add 5-10%:** Strong growth market (Phoenix, Atlanta, Dallas, Houston)
- **No adjustment:** Stable market (small cities, established suburban)
- **Subtract 10-20%:** Declining market (rural, high home ownership)

**Step 4: Factor in Additional Assets**
- Real estate: Add appraised land/building value
- Inventory: Add current inventory at cost
- Accounts receivable: Add collectible AR (commercial WDF clients)

**Example Calculation:**
- Annual Revenue: $250,000
- Operating Expenses: $125,000
- Owner Draws: $75,000
- Net Income: $50,000
- SDE = $50,000 + $75,000 = $125,000
- Equipment Age: 8 years = 4.25x multiple
- Base Value: $125,000 x 4.25 = $531,250
- Market: Phoenix (growing) = +7.5%
- **Fair Market Value: $571,094**
